
# TAUS Fitness Equipments Website

A responsive, mobile-first website developed for TAUS Fitness Equipments, an authorised dealer of Welcare Fitness Equipments in Mangaluru, Karnataka.

## Project Background

This project started with a simple idea: creating a digital experience for customers accessing business information through a QR-based visiting card.

As the idea evolved, I decided to expand the concept into a complete business website for TAUS Fitness Equipments, providing customers with an easier way to explore fitness equipment, browse product categories and contact the business.

## Features

- Responsive, mobile-first design
- Premium black and gold UI
- Product and category-based navigation
- Dedicated pages for different fitness equipment categories
- Home gym and commercial gym equipment sections
- WhatsApp integration for customer enquiries
- Click-to-call functionality
- Instagram integration
- SEO-friendly metadata
- Open Graph social sharing metadata
- Sitemap and robots.txt
- Structured data using Schema.org

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
